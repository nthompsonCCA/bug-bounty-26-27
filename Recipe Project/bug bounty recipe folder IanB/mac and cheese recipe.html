Simple Mac and Cheese Recipe

This simple mac and cheese is very easy to make while still maintaining great flavours. This recipe even has room for changing it up on your own with different cheeses and ingredients added in if you so choose.

Ingredients list

  1. 1 (8 ounce) box elbow macaroni
  2. 1/4 cup butter
  3. 1/4 cup all purpose flour
  4. 1/2 teaspoon salt
  5. ground black pepper
  6. 2 cups milk
  7. 2 cups shredded chedder cheese

Step list

  1. Gather ingredients.
  2. Boil a large pot of lightly salted water, and cook the elbow macaroni in the pot and stirring periodically for around 8 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile, take a sauce pan and melt butter in it at medium heat.
  4. Spend around 5 minutes whisking in flour, salt, and black pepper until its smooth.
  5. Slowly add milk while stirring, spend about 5 minutes doing this while cooking until it is smooth and bubbly.
  6. Add the cheese and stir for about 2-4 minutes until the cheese melts.
  7. Drain the macaroni into the pan and mix it with the cheese.
  8. Serve hot and enjoy the food.
