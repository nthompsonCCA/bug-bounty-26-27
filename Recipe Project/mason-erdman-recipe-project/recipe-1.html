Western Omelette

Insanely good omelette that goes well with cheddar cheese.

They don't normally put cheddar cheese on it, those blasphemous Californians in the West.

Ingredients

  • 3 large eggs
  • 1/4 cup diced ham
  • 1/4 cup chopped green bell pepper
  • 1/4 cup chopped red bell pepper
  • 1/4 cup finely diced onion
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Crack eggs into a bowl, season with salt and pepper, and whisk until fluffy.
  2. Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat.
  3. Add diced ham, onions, and peppers. Sauté until soft and lightly browned.
  4. Pour in whisked eggs and cook undisturbed for 1–2 minutes until the edges begin to set.
  5. Gently lift edges, allowing uncooked egg to flow underneath.
  6. Sprinkle cheese over the surface.
  7. Fold the omelette in half once set and let cook for another minute.
  8. Slide onto a plate and serve hot.
