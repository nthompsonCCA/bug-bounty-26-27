Spaghetti Cacio e Pepe

Home

Spaghetti Cacio e Pepe is a classic Italian pasta dish that combines the simplicity of spaghetti with the rich flavors of Pecorino Romano cheese and black pepper. This recipe is perfect for a quick and satisfying meal.

Prep time: 5 mins.
Cook time: 20 mins.
Servings: 4.

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound spaghetti
  • 6 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 teaspoons ground black pepper
  • 1 3/4 cups Pecorino Romano cheese, grated

Directions:

  1. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook spaghetti in boiling water, stirring occasionally, until tender yet firm to the bite, about 12 minutes. Reserve 1 cup cooking water, then drain spaghetti.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook and stir garlic and pepper in hot oil until fragrant, 1 to 2 minutes. Add cooked spaghetti and Pecorino Romano cheese. Ladle in 1/2 cup reserved cooking water; stir until cheese is melted, about 1 minute. Stir in more cooking water as needed, 1 tablespoon at a time, until sauce coats spaghetti, about 1 minute more.
