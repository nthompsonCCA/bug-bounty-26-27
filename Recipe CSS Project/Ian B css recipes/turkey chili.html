Simple Turkey Chili Recipe

This simple tasty turkey chili recipe is easy to make with lots of flavours. It can be eaten fresh and held leftover so you can enjoy some for multiple days. You can even add some cheese and other toppings onto it for more flavour if you like.

Ingredients list

  1. 1 1/2 teaspoons olive oil
  2. 1 pound ground turkey
  3. 1 onion, chopped
  4. 2 cups water
  5. 1 (28 ounce) can crushed tomatos
  6. 1 (16 ounce) can kidney beans, drained, rinsed, and mashed
  7. 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  8. 2 tablespoon chili powder
  9. 1/2 teaspoon paprika
  10. 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  11. 1/2 teaspoon ground cayenne pepper
  12. 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
  13. 1/2 teaspoon salt
  14. 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper

Step list

  1. Gather your ingredients.
  2. Heat oil in a larger pot over medium heat. Add the turkey and cook and stir evenly for 6-8 minutes until brown. Also stir it in onion until soft.
  3. Add water to the pot. Stir it in tomatoes, kidney beans, and garlic.
  4. Stir it in chili powder, paprika, oregano, cayenne pepper, cumin, salt, and pepper.
  5. Bring the pot to a boil. Reduce the heat to low and let it simmer while covered for around 30 minutes until flavour blends in and the chili thickens.
  6. Enjoy your fresh turkey chili.
