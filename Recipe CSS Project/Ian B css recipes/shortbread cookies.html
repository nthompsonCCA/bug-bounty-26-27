Shortbread Cookies Recipe

These amazing shortbread cookies are extremely simple, anyone could make them. Only 4 ingredients to create these cookies. They are also great for decorating if you so choose to.

Ingredients list

  1. 2 cups of softened butter
  2. 1 cup of white sugar
  3. 2 teaspoons of vanilla extract
  4. 4 cups all purpose flour

Step list

  1. Gather your ingredients.
  2. Preheat your oven to 350 degrees F, or 180 degrees C.
  3. Use an electric mixer and in a large bowl, beat the softened butter and sugar with medium mode until its light and fluffy.
  4. Stir in the vanilla, add flour gradually while mixing until the dough is smooth
  5. Use a cookie press to form cookies with the dough, and place these cookies on the tray. Cook them in the oven for around 10-12 minutes until the edges start to brown.
  6. Remove the cookies from the oven and place them on a cooling rack until they cool down.
  7. Enjoy your fresh cookies.
